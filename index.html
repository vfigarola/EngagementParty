Travel details

Getting here

We recommend flying into Bangor Airport (BGR), it's about a 1 hour drive to Bar Harbor area. You can also fly into Portland (PWM), about 3 hours or Boston (BOS), about 4.5-5 hours.

Plan on renting a car and booking it early. Lodging is spread out, and taxis and rideshares can be hard to find, especially at night.

    Where to stay

    On Mount Desert Island

    Bar Harbor: our pick for most guests. Stay downtown to walk to dinner, ice cream, and the harbor. Below are some options!

    • Bar Harbor Inn & Spa
    • West Street Hotel
    • Harborside Hotel
    • Yellow House Inn
    • Bar Harbor Manor
    • The Inn on Mount Desert
    • Bar Harbor Grand Hotel
    • The Primrose
    • Canterbury Suites B&B
    • Anchorage Motel
    • Acadia Inn
    • Holiday Inn Resort Bar Harbor
    • Acadia Ocean View Hotel

    North Mount Desert Island: Look for cottages, motels, and rentals in Hulls Cove, Salisbury Cove, and Town Hill.

    Northeast Harbor: A quiet sailing village with fewer dinner options. For a splurge, look at The Asticou and ask about a harbor-view room.

    Southwest Harbor and Bass Harbor: Explore the island's quiet side. The Claremont for a waterfront stay or cottages in Bernard and Bass Harbor.

    On the Mainland

    Trenton: Acadia Oceanfront Garden Cottages, Isleview Motel and Cottages, Acadia Gateway Motel and Cottages, Acadia Sunrise Motel, Open Hearth Inn

    Ellsworth: Hampton Inn, Home2 Suites, Comfort Inn, Hawthorn Extended Stay, Acadia Village Resort

    Also check out Airbnb/Vrbo!

    Things to Know

    Book early! July is peak season around Acadia.

    Plan your ride home: Taxis and rideshares can be scarce. If you'll be drinking, arrange a ride or pick a designated driver ahead of time.

    Visiting Acadia? Get a park entrance pass and check the separate timed vehicle reservation if you want to drive up Cadillac Mountain. Save the QR code before you go and check the park's page for the 2027 release schedule.

    Camping? Blackwoods is closest to Bar Harbor, Seawall is on the quiet side, and Schoodic Woods is across the bay. Under the current schedule, most July sites open January 1, 2027, at 10 a.m. Eastern on Recreation.gov. The rest open 14 days before arrival. Near the party, look at Narrows Too or Timberland Acres.
